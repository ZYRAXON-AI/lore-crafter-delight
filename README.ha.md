# Lore Crafter Delight

> Ɗakin karatu na dijital mai motsi — litattafai 60 cikakku don karantawa da sauraro.

**Live app**: https://lore-crafter-delight.lovable.app

## Yadda za a ƙara PDF ko littafinka

1. Yi fork wannan ajiya (ko clone).
2. Ƙirƙiri babban fayil: `pdf-library/<sunanka>/`.
3. Sa a ciki littafinka (`book.txt`, `book.md` ko `book.pdf`), `meta.json` mai `title`, da hoton murfi (`cover.png` / `cover.svg`).
4. Yi commit ka buɗe Pull Request.
5. Bot ɗin haɗawa ta atomatik yana dubawa, littafinka kuma yana bayyana a rukunin yanar gizo cikin mintuna — ba sai an sake tura ba.

## Rubuta da kowane harshe

Ba a iyakance ka da Bengali ko Turanci ba. Aika littafi da kowane harshe da rubutu — Hausa, Larabci, Sinanci, Swahili, ko me. Rukunin yanar gizo na nuna rubutunka kamar yadda ka rubuta.

## Cikakkun dokoki

Karanta cikakkun dokoki mataki-mataki a [`pdf-library/README.md`](pdf-library/README.md). Hoton murfi da `meta.json` wajibi ne.
